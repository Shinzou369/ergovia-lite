Marketing Automation Explained:

Every time you sell an Ergovia-AI subscription, you earn marketing tokens. These tokens are your marketing budget! You can load these tokens into any niche of your choosing to automate its marketing. This means your sales directly fuel your ability to generate more leads and sales, creating a self-sustaining growth loop.

Important: To enable marketing automation for individual niches, you must first activate and configure your overall Ergovia-AI Platform Automation. This involves a one-time setup where you define how our AI agent will handle your personal marketing content distribution and lead engagement.

Ergovia-AI Platform Automation Setup

Configure your marketing AI agent. At least one option from Phase 1 and all of Phase 2 are required.

Phase 1: Content Distribution

How will your marketing content be posted?

By providing API access, our AI agent can directly post marketing content to your selected social media platform, including pages or groups you manage. This offers maximum automation.

If you prefer to maintain direct control, our AI agent will generate ready-to-post content and send it to your preferred channel. You can then copy and paste it into your social media, including groups you participate in.

Phase 2: Lead Engagement & Closing

How will the AI agent communicate with leads?

Our AI agent can engage directly with leads on your chosen messaging platform, follow up, answer questions, and guide them towards closing a sale. Provide details for the platform where you primarily interact with potential customers.
