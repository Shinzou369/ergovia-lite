Telegram Bot Credentials

Get your bot token from @BotFather on Telegram. Format: numbers:letters (35 characters after colon)
Your chat ID where the bot will send messages. Negative for groups, positive for individual chats.
How to get your Telegram credentials

Getting Your Bot Token:

  1. Open Telegram and search for @BotFather
  2. Send /newbot command
  3. Follow the prompts to name your bot
  4. Copy the bot token provided

Getting Your Chat ID:

  1. Add your bot to the group/channel where you want notifications
  2. Send a message in that chat
  3. Visit: https://api.telegram.org/bot[BOT_TOKEN]/getUpdates
  4. Look for the "chat":{"id": value in the response

Appointment Management Setup

The Google Sheet where your appointments are stored. Copy the ID from the URL.
Central sheet for logging all appointments from different sources.
How often to check for appointments needing reminders.
How to get your Google Sheet IDs

Getting Sheet IDs:

  1. Open your Google Sheet in a browser
  2. Look at the URL: https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/[SHEET_ID]/edit
  3. Copy the long string between /d/ and /edit
  4. That's your Sheet ID!

Required Sheet Structure:

Appointments Sheet should have columns:

  • Date, Time, Customer Name, Customer Phone, etc.

Master Sheet will be automatically populated with:

  • Date, Time, Customer Name, Email, Phone, Service, Pet Name, Notes, Status, Created

SMS Service Setup

Your Twilio Account SID for sending SMS messages.
Your Twilio Auth Token (kept secure).
Your Twilio phone number in E.164 format (starting with +1).
How to get your Twilio credentials

Setting up Twilio:

  1. Sign up at twilio.com
  2. Go to Console Dashboard
  3. Find your Account SID and Auth Token
  4. Purchase a phone number in Phone Numbers > Manage > Buy a number
  5. Copy the phone number in E.164 format (+15551234567)

SMS Features:

  • ✅ Instant appointment confirmations
  • ⏰ Automated reminder messages
  • 📱 Professional business messaging
  • 🔔 Cancellation policy notifications

Calendly Integration

Personal Access Token from your Calendly developer settings.
How to get your Calendly Personal Access Token

Getting Calendly API Access:

  1. Log in to your Calendly account
  2. Go to calendly.com/integrations/api_webhooks
  3. Click "Generate New Token"
  4. Copy the Personal Access Token
  5. Paste it in the field above

Webhook Setup:
